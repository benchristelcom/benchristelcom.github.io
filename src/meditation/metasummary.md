# Meditation Instructions

Your mind's natural inclination is to rise up like a hot air balloon to the highest mental state, unless something is pulling it back down.

You don't need to *do* anything to be happy.

Meditation is *undoing* of the stress-making process. It happens on its own if you just sit.

This page describes *one possible* meditation technique that has worked for me.

1. **Sit** in an upright, relaxed position.
   - Just sit normally. The #1 mistake here is being too tight or trying to have "good posture." Meditation is not a postural exercise.
2. Establish **mindful awareness**. Hear all the sounds, feel all the sensations going on right now. After five minutes, close your eyes.
3. **Buddha's smile**: relax your eyes into a peaceful, kind, slight smile like you see on Buddha statues. This helps create **wholesome and effortless intentions**.
4. **Anapanasati**: be aware of expansion and contraction (e.g. breath sensations) and where they are appearing in the body. But no need to focus or try to control attention.
5. **Let go** of tension.
   - As you meditate, tensions will arise in your mind/body.
   - They feel a bit like a clenched fist, or like the "fabric" of your mind is being pulled through a small opening.
   - Tensions are caused by clinging to a sensation or thought.
   - When you notice tension, relax with an exhale and remind yourself that it's okay to let the thought/sensation drop. Then just dispassionately observe the thoughts / tension until they go away on their own.
6. After detecting tension, refresh your **awareness** and **wholesome/effortless smile**.
