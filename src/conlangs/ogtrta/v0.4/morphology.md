# Suggested Morphology

## Verb-to-noun derivation

- `#GER`
- `#INF`
- `#QUALITY` - inverse of `#FUL`.
- `#RESULT`
- `#INPUT`
- `#AGENT`
- `#INSTRUMENT`
- `#PLACE`

## Noun-to-verb derivation

These need not exist as affixes, since they can be trivially paraphrased.

- `#FUL` - **respect** &rarr; **respectful**
- `#RELATED` - **sol** &rarr; **solar**; **syntax** &rarr; **syntactic**
- `#MAKE` - valence 1: *Y#MAKE X* &rarr; "make X into Y"
- `#CREATE` - valence 0
- `#APPLY` - **corona** &rarr; **coronate**
- `#ACTAS` - **parent** &rarr; **to parent**
- `#BECOME`
- `#BE`

## Verb-to-verb derivation

- `#MAKE` - **deep** &rarr; **deepen**
- `#BECOME` - **red** &rarr; **redden**
